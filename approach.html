Our approach

A practical framework for moving forward.

Good project coordination starts with a shared understanding of the requirement and a clear view of the decisions ahead.

The stages below describe an intended engagement framework. Actual procedures, project controls and responsibilities will vary by project and must be agreed in writing.

01 / DISCOVER

Requirement Understanding

Clarify objectives, users, context, scope and constraints.

02 / PLAN

Initial Planning

Identify key inputs, priorities and the next decisions required.

03 / COORDINATE

Design Coordination

Bring relevant design and technical inputs together through the agreed team.

04 / PREPARE

Development Preparation

Confirm scope, responsibilities, approvals and project arrangements.

05 / PROGRESS

Execution Coordination

Coordinate agreed activities and communicate progress appropriately.

06 / REVIEW

Quality Review

Review work against documented requirements and agreed checks.

07 / CLOSE

Handover and Support

Complete the agreed handover documentation and close-out activities.
